Sourcing Strategy

Two paths — matched to your role.

The right approach depends on role seniority, market scarcity, and urgency — often, it's both at once.

Passive

Advertise & Attract

  • High-volume job boards for maximum reach and stronger shortlisting control
  • Careers page promotion shared across professional networks
  • Best for well-defined roles with a strong likely applicant pool
Proactive

Search & Headhunt

  • Direct market mapping and targeted search via professional networks
  • Warm outreach to passive candidates who aren't actively looking
  • Best for senior, niche, or historically hard-to-fill roles

Screening

Salary expectations

Work rights

Discretion When It Matters

Confidential & executive searches.

For sensitive or senior appointments, discretion protects everyone involved. Target candidates are approached by an independent contact who does not disclose the engaging organisation until the candidate confirms genuine interest — at which point they're asked to sign an NDA before any further detail is shared.

  • No employer name disclosed upfront
  • NDA-protected once a candidate opts in
  • Ideal for replacing a current incumbent discreetly
  • Protects candidates who are currently employed
